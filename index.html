01 · The proposition

History does not disappear.
It travels.

Bangladesh's present cannot be explained by a lack of intelligence, creativity or dignity. Historical ecological vulnerability, colonial rule, Partition, displacement, political struggle, unequal access to resources and changing global economic relationships can leave forms of insecurity that travel across generations.

The question is not simply “How do we become richer?”

It is “How do we expand the meaningful choices available to every generation?”

06 · AI and reality

AI can reconnect reality.
It can also compress it.

A civilizational AI should not become a machine that declares one final truth about Bangladesh. It should help people discover evidence, contradictions, missing voices and relationships.

Decompress reality

Connect archives, oral histories, maps, films, economic records, environmental data and competing interpretations. Preserve provenance, uncertainty and disagreement.

Compress reality

Reduce complex people and histories into one narrative, one metric, one algorithmic recommendation or one supposedly universal truth.

The purpose of AI is not to replace human judgment.
It is to expand human capacity to understand, question and choose.
07 · The question

What should a child inherit?

Not only the memory of everything previous generations survived, but the freedom to choose a future previous generations could not.

A generation should not have to reproduce the suffering of the previous generation simply to prove that it has survived.

More knowledge

Historical literacy, scientific thinking, financial capability and critical reasoning.

More choices

Education, productive work, ownership, mobility, safety and time.

More agency

The ability to participate in family, community, institutions and the future of the civilization.
